# PF-Q2-14 · Event context for real time: camera type, aircraft type, entity

- **Owner:** Maksym Ch. · **Agents:** pipeline-architect (+ gm-tracker-engineer for the ported rules) · **Status:** todo · **Quarter:** Q2, pulled forward for PF-Q2-07
- **Deps:** ADR-004 · **Related:** PF-Q1-03 (stage detector), ADR-002 (camera type in real time), PF-Q1-16 (GM v2 `VideoContext`)

## Why

Which checks run on a camera depends on two facts about the event: the camera type (cone or wing) and the aircraft type
(aircraft or jet).

The client split (`docs/05_module_logic.md`):
- aircraft: 12 checks on both cameras, 18 on the cone camera only;
- jets: 3 checks on both cameras, 13 on the cone camera only, 8 on the wing camera only;
- some checks do not exist for jets: steering pin, both handrail checks, walk-arounds, pin verification.

Production decides both facts inside GM, after the whole video. In real time they must be decided early and causally,
with a known decision frame.

## Today

- **Camera type.**
  - Production GM classifies the camera in its second pass over the whole video.
  - Ported as a buffered single-pass decision (ADR-002, `pf/gm/buffered.py`, `pf/gm/camera.py`):
    - the classifier runs on every 8th frame with an aircraft;
    - the decision comes 50 votes after T_ARR (≈49.5 s);
    - probabilities are bit-identical to the production rule;
    - cost: 0.9–1.6 ms per frame.
- **Aircraft type.**
  - Production GM counts per-frame votes `airplane` / `jet` (`external/general_model_prod/main.py:741-935`) and writes
    `airplane_type` into the GM report (`:1127`).
  - Ported as an accumulator with `decided_at` (more than 500 votes) in `pf/gm/context.py`.
  - beltloader-chocks and hand-signals recompute the type locally.
- **Entity (airline).** Decided after 40 detector hits (`pf/gm/context.py`). No module reads it; it only goes into the report.
- **Test-set coverage.** The test-set plan carries an aircraft type for 23 of 69 events (14 JET, 9 AIRCRAFT). The rest have
  none.
- **Existing gating idea.** The August stage study (`G:\gat_stages\IMPLEMENTATION.md`, phase 2) already proposes gating by
  camera and aircraft type as deterministic metadata.

## Scope

1. **Context component (ADR-004).** Owns three fields per camera session: camera type, aircraft type, entity. For each
   field it keeps the value, `decided_at` and `source`, and emits `CONTEXT_DECIDED` / `CONTEXT_REVISED` events.
2. **Camera type.** The camera registry is the primary source, because a camera does not move between cone and wing
   positions. The classifier runs as a check and raises a mismatch. Where no registry exists, the classifier decides alone.
3. **Aircraft type early enough for routing.**
   - measure the decision frame against T_ARR;
   - define what the modules do before the decision: wait, or run both variants.
4. **Routing table.** Aircraft type × camera → checks, taken from the client split. The gating table of PF-Q1-03 consumes it.
