## Key points
1. **A GM row = exactly 6 elements `[x1,y1,x2,y2,conf,cls]`** — all modules unpack it with star-unpacking, `hand-signals` also positionally; an extra column silently breaks xyxy. `conf` is actually read only by `aircraft-chocks` and `seat-belts`.
2. **The minimum of GM v2 classes:** 26 "decisive" (`trailer/gse/pushback` in 22–23 modules) + 3 "cosmetic" (`airplane`, `roi`, `safety_zone`); 7 modules do a reverse lookup via `str2id` → **an unknown id = a crash**. The synthesized `obstacle`/`side_obstacle` are read as detections in 8 modules.
3. **Tracker contract:** the wrapper `{tr_id, xyxy, cls_str, conf, state_dict, data.bl_type}`; the classes read are `airplane`(25), `person`(14), `beltloader`(11), `gse`(3). **The `state_dict` key set is fixed in both directions** — `from_state_dict` on the 10 cv_common pins fails on unknown keys (the `_bl_type_*` incident). Three variants of the aircraft schema have already been observed: 38 / 40 / 39 (`_height_mode`).
4. **Coupling to the tracker internals:** `_p0/_st/_of_dots_lifetime/_init_dots_lifetime` — 10 modules (arrival stage block), `_status` — 10, `_obj_id` — 8, `_color` — 6, `_xyxy` — 5; `gse-chocks` reads 4 more private thresholds.
5. **Local copies of events:** the arrival stage block is duplicated in 9 modules (twice in `safety-zone`), although the tracker already emits `have_arrival_stage`/`have_pre_arrival_stage`; `pushback_attached` — 2; T_arr/T_dep from the status — 7; the equality `frame_number == arrival_frame` — 4 (fragile with respect to X2); BL@door/BL leave locally — 9.
6. **Pixels** for the decision — 15 modules; **own models** — 13 (discrepancies with `module_map.json` for hand-signals/handrails/seat-belts/safety-vests/wing-walkers — fix the cards); **two-pass** — 4 (`aircraft-chocks`, `pin-verification`, `hand-signals`, `safety-zone`); 5 know the video length.
7. **An empty frame `[]` → 15 modules do not update the aircraft track** — gap-fill with placeholder frames (X2) must take this into account: either the placeholder frame carries the last aircraft track, or we accept the degradation.
8. **Pins:** cv_common 10 different (`dd5b5547`×12, then 9 others), db_worker 4; `fps=8` is hard-coded in 5 modules.

## Consequences for v2 (recorded in ADR-001)
- GM v2 must produce the same 6-element row and the same ids, including the synthesized 25/29/30/31 and the class-2 aircraft row (v1-compat writer).
- Tracker v2 cannot add keys to `state_dict` for the old modules → new fields only in `data{}` or in the frame fields `stage/events/anchors` (nobody reads them) + a separate `schema_version` for new consumers.
- Module porting: first replace the 9 copies of arrival-stage and the 4 `frame_number ==` equalities with `anchors` — this is the first real step of "modules on the fly".
