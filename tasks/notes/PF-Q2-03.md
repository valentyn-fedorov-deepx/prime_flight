(`--no-video`), and is already measured on the gat-streaming stand (test bench): **recall 83 %, precision 91 % on 29 videos with all 12 violations**
(`docs/streaming_ref/modules.md`, `04_modules.md` → M04). Client logic (`05_module_logic.md`): Pass — all BLs parked
and chocked, the chock stays while the BL is stationary; Fail — at least one BL stopped without a chock; NO — no BL stops / an obstacle; NO-obst — FOV blocked.

Hedge: does not depend on GM/Tracker changes for RT, so it can be demonstrated to the client even if PF-Q2-02 is late.
